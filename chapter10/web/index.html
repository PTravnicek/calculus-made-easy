Geometrical Meaning of Differentiation

A narrated, animated walk through Silvanus Thompson's chapter on what $\frac{dy}{dx}$ looks like on a graph. The video stops to ask you questions along the way, and Thompson's Exercises VIII wait underneath.

After the video

Exercises VIII

Thompson's ten exercises for this chapter. Type a number, a fraction such as -5/3, a root such as sqrt(2), or an angle such as 26°34'. Thompson's own advice applies: check what you deduce by plotting the curves.

Slope lab

From chord to tangent

Part 1 in your own hands. Drag $Q$ along the curve and shrink $dx$: the slope of the chord $QT$ settles on the slope of the tangent. The lower graph plots $\frac{dy}{dx}$ against $x$, as in Part 6.

Functions you can use: sqrt, sin, cos, tan, exp, ln, abs, and ^ for powers.
